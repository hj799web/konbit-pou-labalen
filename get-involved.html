Get Involved

Reconnect with Labalèn

If you grew up outside Haiti and your parents or grandparents are from Labalèn, you can help with the skills, time, and family connections you already have.

Translate and share updates

Help translate project updates into Haitian Creole, English, French, or Portuguese. Share updates with family and friends so they can follow the work.

Keep family stories alive

Ask an older relative what they remember about Labalèn. With their permission, record a conversation or write down a story your family wants to preserve.

Bring a skill or a local idea

Help with photography, communication, fundraising, or organizing an online conversation. If you live in Labalèn, tell KPL what would make daily life easier.
