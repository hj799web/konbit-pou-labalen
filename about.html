Labalèn, Haiti

Where our families come from

Labalèn is home to the people who live there today and part of the family history of many who live abroad. Konbit Pou Labalèn brings those ties into the work of improving daily life in the community.

Our roots in Labalèn

Many families abroad first got to know Labalèn through stories from their parents and grandparents. Konbit Pou Labalèn gives the next generation a way to reconnect and take part.

Open the family-roots artwork

What is a konbit?

A konbit is a Haitian tradition of working together for the community. KPL puts that tradition into practice by bringing residents and diaspora families together around priorities identified in Labalèn.

How projects take shape

We start by listening to residents, then look for ways people at home and abroad can contribute time, skills, materials, or funding. We’ll share new projects here as local priorities and plans take shape.
